# Sales Kit — Direct Invoicing

No storefront. An agency says yes, you invoice, they pay, you deliver. Ten minutes per sale.

---

## Setup — once, ~20 minutes

**PayPal Business** (primary). Free to open, works from Jordan, agencies recognise it and can pay by card without a PayPal account. Invoicing is built in: Send & Request → Create invoice.

**Wise Business** (secondary). Better rates, real bank details in USD, lower fees on larger amounts. Some agencies prefer a bank transfer to a card payment. Also free.

Offer both and let the buyer choose — it removes a reason to stall.

**Fees, for context:** PayPal invoicing runs roughly 3.5% + fixed fee on international payments, so about $30–35 on a $900 sale. Wise is usually less. Both beat Lemon Squeezy's 5% + 50¢, and all of them beat Gumroad's 10%.

---

## The license key

Generate one per buyer and record it. Format:

```
PPCOT-2026-0001-AG
PPCOT-2026-0002-AG
```

`PPCOT` = product · year · sequential buyer number · your initials.

Keep a private log (not in project knowledge):

| Key | Agency | Contact | Email | Date | Amount | Method |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

This is what makes the license enforceable — a leaked copy traces to a number, and you can say whose it was.

---

## The flow

1. Agency says yes
2. Send the invoice (template below), attach nothing yet
3. Payment lands — PayPal/Wise emails you
4. Send the delivery email with the zip attached (460KB, fits any inbox) and their license key
5. Log the key
6. Diarise a check-in for 7 days later

## Refunds

14 days, no questions, as promised. Refund through the same channel, mark the key revoked in your log, and don't argue. At ten sales the goodwill is worth more than the $900, and the reason they give you is free product research.

## Tax note

You're a US citizen, so this income is reportable to the IRS regardless of where you live, and Jordan may tax it too. Keep every invoice and the log above. One conversation with a US expat tax accountant once sales start — not before.
